This Cookie Policy explains how Nasil Supply & Logistics (“NASIL”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember preferences, and support security and performance.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies primarily for purposes that are necessary to operate our website and services, including:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: required for core site functionality, such as page navigation, form submissions, and secure areas of the site.
  • Security and session cookies: used to help protect the site and manage user sessions (for example, when logging in to customer or staff portals) and to support protections such as CSRF/security mechanisms.
  • Preference cookies (where applicable): used to remember certain settings or choices to improve your experience.

At this time, we do not intentionally use cookies for advertising profiling on this website. If we introduce non-essential cookies (such as analytics or marketing cookies) in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, provide appropriate notice or choices.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some pages may load resources from third-party providers (for example, fonts or libraries). These providers may set their own cookies or collect technical information according to their own policies. We encourage you to review the privacy and cookie policies of any third-party services you interact with.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect how the website functions, including access to secure areas or the ability to submit forms.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
