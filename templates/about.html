Who We Are

Nasil Supply & Logistics is a Kampala, Uganda–based supply and logistics company focused on moving goods, materials, and supplies reliably for organizations across multiple sectors.

From procurement and sourcing to storage, transport, distribution, and import & export coordination, our team works closely with clients to keep operations efficient, dependable, and responsive. We support local, regional, and international requirements—serving clients worldwide.
