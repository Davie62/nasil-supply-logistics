Where We Work

NASIL provides supply and logistics services to clients across the globe. From our base in Kampala, Uganda, we support local, regional, and international requirements—helping organizations source, move, store, and deliver goods wherever they operate.

Whether you need procurement and sourcing, supply solutions, warehousing coordination, transport and distribution, or import & export support, we work with you to plan the right approach for your origin, destination, and timeline.

What we coordinate

  • International procurement and sourcing support
  • Cross-border and worldwide shipment coordination
  • Warehousing, handling, and distribution planning
  • Import & export logistics support

From Kampala to the World

Our team coordinates operations from Kampala, Uganda, supporting clients and deliveries across international destinations as required.

Flexible by Shipment & Destination

Routes, transit times, and handling depend on the destination, shipment type, and applicable requirements—we tailor solutions accordingly.

End-to-End Coordination

From planning and sourcing to movement and delivery support, we help keep your supply chain organized and responsive.
