Nasil Supply & Logistics (“NASIL”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, request a quote, track a shipment, or use our customer or staff portals.

This policy is provided for general informational purposes and may be updated. For specific legal or compliance advice, please consult a qualified professional.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact and inquiry details: name, email address, phone number, company/organization, and the content of messages you send through our contact or quote forms.
  • Quote and service information: details you provide about goods, origin/destination, timelines, and other requirements needed to prepare quotations or coordinate services.
  • Account and portal information: login credentials and profile-related information if you register for or use our customer or staff portals.
  • Shipment and tracking information: tracking references and related status information you submit or view when using our tracking features.
  • Technical and usage information: basic information such as IP address, browser/device details, and server logs collected automatically for security and operational purposes.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries, provide quotations, and deliver requested services.
  • Coordinate supply, logistics, and shipment-related activities, including tracking and updates.
  • Operate, maintain, and secure our website, portals, and systems.
  • Communicate with you about your requests, accounts, or services (subject to your preferences where applicable).
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information when necessary to:

  • Provide and coordinate services (for example, with relevant partners or service providers involved in sourcing, transport, or delivery, as applicable to your request).
  • Operate our systems with trusted service providers (such as hosting, communications, or IT support), subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • Comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect the rights, safety, and security of NASIL, our users, and others.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide services, maintain business and operational records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or regulatory requirements.

5. Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, security, and session management. For more information, please see our Cookie Policy.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of certain information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request or ask questions about your information, please contact us using the details below.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.
